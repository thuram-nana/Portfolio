Who is Junior Thuram Nana?

Junior Thuram Nana is a self-taught software engineer and security researcher based in Buea, South-West Region, Cameroon. He is the founder and Managing Director of SIGIL SARL, a GovTech / RegTech company.

SIGIL SARL (Sovereign Integrity Governance Infrastructure Labs) builds sovereign governance infrastructure for institutions: public institutions, banks and other critical institutions, and private companies. The guiding idea is control: the institution holds the cryptographic keys, its data stays under its control, and every consequential action is authorized, bounded, and proven. The company's systems are VIGIL (a governed cybersecurity and evidence platform), RÉCOR (a beneficial-ownership registry) and APEX (anti-corruption intelligence for public procurement). Separately, Junior has built software across mobile, web, and backend systems.
