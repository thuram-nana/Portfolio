SIGIL SARL · governed cybersecurity & evidence platform

VIGIL PAID PILOT

A governed cybersecurity and evidence platform. Work flows Protect → Assess → Lead-vs-fact → Remediate → Dossier. In the Lead-vs-fact stage a deterministic component called the Oracle re-verifies each observation before it is treated as a fact; confirmed facts are signed and remain checkable offline. An engagement closes with one tamper-evident dossier that re-verifies offline, so a reader does not have to trust SIGIL to check the result. VIGIL Sovereign deploys on-premise or in the client's own cloud — the client holds the keys and the data, SIGIL has no default access and receives no telemetry. VIGIL SaaS is a managed offering in preparation.
