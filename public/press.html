Long bio (150 words)

Junior Thuram Nana is a self-taught software engineer and security researcher based in Buea, South-West Region, Cameroon. In 2023 he founded SIGIL SARL (Sovereign Integrity Governance Infrastructure Labs), a GovTech / RegTech company where he is Managing Director.

SIGIL builds sovereign governance infrastructure for institutions — public institutions, banks and other critical institutions, and private companies. The institution holds the cryptographic keys, its data stays under its control, and every consequential action is authorized, bounded, and proven. The company runs three systems: VIGIL, a governed cybersecurity and evidence platform now in paid pilot; RÉCOR, a beneficial-ownership registry built to the Beneficial Ownership Data Standard and demonstrated to Open Ownership in 2026; and APEX, anti-corruption intelligence for public procurement.

Separately, Junior has built software across mobile, web, and backend systems. His security work includes responsible disclosures to Meta / WhatsApp (2024) and Samsung (2025).
